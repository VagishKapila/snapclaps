1. Introduction

Welcome to SnapClaps (operated by Varshyl Inc), a travel deal curation platform under the brand OopsLuxEscapes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website (snapclaps.com) and social media channels.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. By using our website and services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you interact with SnapClaps, we may collect:

  • Email Address: If you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us
  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, or messages sent through our contact form
  • Social Media Profiles: Your follower status or interactions on TikTok, Instagram, or other platforms
  • Preferences: Travel interests, destination preferences, deal types you're interested in

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device information
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on each page, links clicked, referral source
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location (based on IP address, not GPS)
  • Interaction Data: Scroll depth, button clicks, form submissions, video plays

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Affiliate Networks: CJ Affiliates, Travelpayouts, Villiers Jets, ShareASale, Impact, Awin, Rakuten (conversion data, click tracking)
  • Analytics Platforms: Google Analytics, Hotjar, or similar tools
  • Social Media Platforms: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube (engagement metrics, follower counts)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

3.1 Service Delivery

  • Delivering travel deals and content to your email or social media feed
  • Responding to your inquiries and customer support requests
  • Managing your newsletter subscription preferences
  • Processing affiliate commission tracking and referrals

3.2 Improvement & Analytics

  • Analyzing user behavior to improve our website and content
  • Testing new features and optimizing the user experience
  • Understanding which deals resonate with our audience
  • Measuring campaign effectiveness and ROI

3.3 Marketing & Personalization

  • Sending promotional emails about new deals and travel tips (with your consent)
  • Creating personalized content recommendations
  • Segment audiences for targeted travel content

3.4 Legal & Compliance

  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations (GDPR, CCPA, FTC affiliate requirements)
  • Detecting and preventing fraud or abuse
  • Protecting the security and integrity of our platform

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience:

4.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality and security
  • Performance Cookies: Track usage patterns to optimize performance
  • Tracking Cookies: Affiliate tracking cookies (to attribute commissions to deal sources)
  • Marketing Cookies: Enable retargeting ads and social media integration

4.2 Third-Party Cookies

Third-party platforms we work with may place their own cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics: Tracks website traffic and user behavior
  • Affiliate Networks: Track your clicks and bookings for commission attribution
  • Social Media Platforms: Integrate TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube content

4.3 Cookie Consent & Opt-Out

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Be aware that disabling certain cookies may limit website functionality. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Partners

Important: SnapClaps contains links to third-party websites (airline booking sites, hotel platforms, travel agencies, etc.). When you click these links and make a purchase, we may receive an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you.

5.1 Our Affiliate Partners

We are an affiliate partner with the following networks and brands:

  • CJ Affiliates — Flight and hotel bookings
  • Travelpayouts — Multi-supplier travel booking platform
  • Villiers Jets — Private jet charter and membership
  • ShareASale — Merchant-specific affiliate programs
  • Impact — Performance marketing partnerships
  • Awin — Global affiliate marketing network
  • Rakuten — Cashback and rewards partnerships

5.2 Affiliate Link Disclosure

Affiliate links may be visually marked or clearly disclosed at the point of use. When you click an affiliate link, tracking cookies are placed on your device to attribute your booking to SnapClaps. This helps us earn a commission while helping you discover amazing deals.

5.3 Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our affiliate partners and linked websites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices. Please review their privacy policies before making a purchase or providing personal information.

6. Analytics & Data Processing

We use analytics tools to understand how users interact with our platform:

6.1 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to track website traffic, user behavior, and engagement. Google may place cookies on your device and process data about your visits. You can opt out at: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout/

6.2 Hotjar (Heatmaps & Session Recording)

We may use Hotjar to record sessions and track user interactions. This helps us understand how users navigate our site. Hotjar may collect information about your device, location, and interaction patterns. You can opt out at: https://www.hotjar.com/privacy/

6.3 Data Aggregation & Anonymization

Most analytics data is aggregated and anonymized before analysis. We do not link personally identifiable information to analytics profiles unless you explicitly provide it (e.g., through a contact form).

7. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all website traffic
  • Secure storage of email lists and personal data
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Limited access to personal data (only authorized team members)
  • Secure deletion of data when no longer needed
Note on Security: While we implement robust security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

8.1 GDPR Rights (EU Residents)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Opt out of marketing communications and data processing
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

8.2 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: Request what personal information we collect about you
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

8.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, contact us at hello@snapclaps.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days (or as required by applicable law). Please include:

  • Your name and email address
  • The specific right you are requesting (access, deletion, correction, etc.)
  • Any relevant details about your interaction with us
