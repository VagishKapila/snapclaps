Maldives Honeymoon: Overwater Bungalows & Smart Budget Hacks

📅 March 21, 2026 | 11 min read

Why the Maldives?

The Maldives is the #1 honeymoon destination. Overwater bungalows, pristine coral, water so clear you see fish from your deck. But it's expensive. Budget resorts start $300/night, luxury hits $3,000+.

Best Time to Go (and Save 50%)

Dry season (November-March) is peak with wedding-season prices. Save 40-50% by visiting April-October (monsoon season with occasional rain, fewer tourists). May-August is cheapest. Flights: $600-900 return from US coast.

Where to Stay

Budget Resorts ($300-500/night)

Island resorts without overwater bungalows offer similar experiences for less. You swim from a beach instead of your deck. Resorts like Angsana Velavaru offer great value with water activities included.

Mid-Range ($600-1,000/night)

A few overwater bungalows with all-inclusive meals and activities. Perfect for honeymoons. You get the overwater experience, house reef snorkeling, and 2-3 meals daily included.

Luxury (Book Low Season)

Six Senses, Conrad, Four Seasons cost $2,000-5,000/night in peak. In August, same resorts drop to $800-1,200. That's when to splurge on luxury.

💡 Hack: Book overwater bungalows for nights 1 & last in July/August (cheapest), island bungalows for mid-stay to save $1,000+ while keeping romantic overwater bookends.

Activities

  • House Reef Snorkeling: Free from your resort. Incredible visibility, fish everywhere. Bring your own gear ($20 savings).
  • Diving: $60-100/dive if certified. $400-600 for certification.
  • Dolphin Cruise: $30-50/person, magical experience.
  • Local Island Tours: $40-80, visit inhabited islands, eat with locals.

Food & All-Inclusive Breakdown

All-inclusive plans ($100-200/person/day) include meals and alcohol. Worth it since restaurants charge resort markups (mains $30-50). Eliminate the shock of $200 dinner bills by booking all-inclusive upfront.

Budget Breakdown: 5-7 Days

  • Flights (US-Maldives): $700-900/person
  • Resort mid-range (5 nights): $3,500-5,000
  • Diving/activities: $200-400
  • Boat transfers: $100-200
  • Spa/extras: $200-500
  • Total for 2: $5,500-8,500

A luxury honeymoon for under $9k. The Maldives is cheaper than most think if you book the right season and choose mid-range resorts.
