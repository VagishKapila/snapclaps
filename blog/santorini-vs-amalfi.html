Santorini vs Amalfi Coast: Which Should You Visit?

📅 March 25, 2026 | 9 min read

The Quick Answer

Santorini: Famous sunsets, blue domes, crowded, expensive.

Amalfi: Dramatic cliffs, lemon trees, fewer crowds, cheaper.

Santorini Deep Dive

Santorini is Instagram paradise. The whitewashed buildings + blue domes against the caldera are iconic. But June-August it's PACKED. 3-4 million tourists to a 15,000-person island. You're waiting in line at restaurants, hiking in 100°F heat with thousands of people, paying €35 for a gyro.

Prices: Hotels $150-250/night (basic). Restaurants €20-35/main. A sunset dinner: €80/person.

Best for: Instagram, nightlife, all-night parties.

Amalfi Coast Deep Dive

The Amalfi Coast is 30 miles of vertical cliffs, colorful villages (Positano, Ravello, Atrani), lemon trees, and pasta with seafood. Fewer day-trippers because you have to stay nearby (it's not a quick boat ride from Athens). The road is insane (hairpin turns, narrow), keeping some tourists away.

Prices: Hotels €80-150/night. Restaurants €15-25/main. Same sunset dinner: €40/person.

Best for: Hiking, food, quiet villages, nature.

The Verdict

Choose Santorini if: You want iconic Instagram photos, don't mind crowds, have limited time, and want nightlife/luxury resorts.

Choose Amalfi if: You want authenticity, fewer tourists, better food, lower prices, and don't need the "famous sunset" to enjoy yourself.

Pro Tip

Visit both in May or September. Skip July-August entirely for Greece (40°C heat, max crowds). May and September have 25°C weather, 40% fewer tourists, and lower prices. Santorini + Amalfi = 1-week dream trip for €1,200-1,500/person (flights + hotel + food).
