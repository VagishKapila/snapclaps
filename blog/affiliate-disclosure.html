1. What Are Affiliate Links?

An affiliate link is a special URL that tracks when you click it and make a booking through a partner website. When you use our link to book a flight, hotel, or travel experience, the merchant (airline, booking platform, hotel chain, etc.) recognizes that you came from SnapClaps and may pay us a commission.

Example: You see a deal for "Paris flights from $289" on SnapClaps. You click the link, go to the booking platform, and complete your purchase. The booking platform pays SnapClaps a commission (typically 1-10% of the base booking value, depending on the partner). Your price remains exactly the same—the commission comes from the merchant, not from your pocket.

2. How We Earn Commissions

2.1 Commission Model

SnapClaps participates in performance-based affiliate programs. Here's how it works:

  • You click our link → A tracking cookie is placed on your device
  • You complete a booking → The merchant recognizes the sale came from SnapClaps
  • We receive a commission → Typically 1-10% of the base booking value (varies by partner)
  • Your price = retail price → You pay the same amount whether you click our link or go directly

2.2 Commission Rates Vary

Commission rates differ by partner and product type:

  • Flight bookings: Typically 1-3% commission
  • Hotel bookings: Typically 2-8% commission
  • Tour & activities: Typically 3-10% commission
  • Car rentals: Typically 2-5% commission
  • Travel insurance: Typically 5-20% commission
  • Premium products (private jets, luxury travel): Up to 30% on some products

Commissions are earned only if you complete a booking. Clicks without purchases generate no revenue for SnapClaps.

5. No Extra Cost to You

Critical Point: Affiliate commissions do NOT increase your booking costs. You pay exactly the same price whether you click our link or go directly to the booking platform.

Why? Affiliate commissions come from the merchant's marketing budget, not from customer prices. Airlines, hotels, and booking platforms set their prices independently. When you book through our link, the merchant simply credits SnapClaps with the referral.

5.1 Price Comparison

  • Booking via SnapClaps affiliate link: $289 per flight
  • Booking directly on airline website: $289 per flight
  • Booking via different affiliate: $289 per flight

The price is identical. The only difference: the merchant may pay SnapClaps a commission for the referral.

6. Does Affiliation Impact Our Recommendations?

6.1 Our Commitment to Objectivity

SnapClaps is primarily a deal curation platform. We search for real, verified deals across multiple booking platforms and present them to our audience. Our primary goal is to find incredible value for travelers, not to maximize our affiliate commissions.

6.2 Deal Selection Process

We select deals based on:

  • Savings percentage: How much money you save vs. regular prices
  • Deal authenticity: Is it a real deal or an error fare that might be cancelled?
  • Desirability: Is this a destination or experience our audience wants?
  • Booking quality: Can you actually complete the booking without issues?

We do NOT prioritize deals based on which affiliate partner pays the highest commission.

6.3 Partner Selection

We partner with major, reputable travel booking platforms (Booking.com, Expedia, etc.) because they are trusted by millions and offer competitive pricing—not because they pay particularly high commissions. In fact, some of our best partners pay relatively modest commissions because their value to customers is already strong.

7. FTC Compliance & Legal

7.1 FTC Endorsement Guides

SnapClaps complies with the Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides, which require:

  • Clear & Conspicuous Disclosure: Affiliate relationships must be easy to spot
  • Upfront Honesty: We disclose before you click
  • No Deceptive Practices: We don't hide affiliate relationships or use misleading CTAs
  • Accurate Claims: We don't exaggerate savings or make false promises about deals

7.2 FTC Act, Section 5

Section 5 of the FTC Act prohibits unfair or deceptive practices. SnapClaps commits to:

  • Disclosing material connections (affiliate relationships) clearly
  • Not using misleading language or hidden disclosures
  • Not making claims we cannot substantiate
  • Honoring our affiliate disclosures consistently across all channels

7.3 FTC Staff Guidance on Social Media Disclosures

For TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube content, we follow FTC guidance:

  • #ad or #sponsored: Clearly mark content where we benefit financially
  • Disclosure placement: Disclosures appear before viewers are encouraged to click
  • Platform native tools: We use platform-native disclosure features (TikTok brand partnerships, Instagram links)

SnapClaps believes in full transparency regarding our business model and affiliate relationships. Here's our pledge to you:

  • Open about affiliate income: We disclose that we earn commissions
  • Clear about what affects recommendations: We explain that deals are chosen by value, not commission
