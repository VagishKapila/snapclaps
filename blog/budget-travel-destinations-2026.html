10 Best Budget Travel Destinations for 2026

Published April 13, 2026 · 12-minute read

Affordable Flights + Cheap Living: Where to Travel Without Emptying Your Wallet

Traveling doesn't have to cost $10,000. Some destinations let you fly cheaply AND live cheaply. Your $1,500 budget covers roundtrip flights from the US coast plus 10 days of hotels, food, and activities.

Here are 10 destinations where that's actually possible in 2026.

1. 🇹🇭 Bangkok, Thailand

Bangkok

Why: Southeast Asia's cheapest major city. Insanely good street food, temples, nightlife, islands within reach.

Roundtrip flights from LAX: $480–$620 (watch for Aviasales deals)

Best time to visit: November–February (cool and dry)

$15–20/day budget (hostels, street food)
$40–60/day comfortable travel

What to do: Grand Palace, Wat temples, floating markets, island day trips (Koh Chang, Koh Samui), street food tours, muay thai training.

Pro tip: Ignore Patpong. Go to Chinatown (Yaowarat) and the old city for real Bangkok. Hostels in Khao San Road are cheap and full of travelers.

2. 🇲🇽 Mexico City, Mexico

Mexico City (CDMX)

Why: Cheap flights from the US, walkable neighborhoods, amazing food, world-class museums.

Roundtrip flights from DFW: $180–$300 (often finds deals under $250)

Best time to visit: March–May or September–November (clear skies, fewer tourists than Dec)

$20–30/day budget
$50–80/day comfortable

What to do: Frida Kahlo Museum, Museo Antropológico, Templo Mayor, street tacos, Xochimilco (floating gardens), neighborhoods like Condesa and Roma.

Pro tip: The metro costs $0.25/ride. Stay in Roma or Condesa for walkability. Avoid taxis; use Uber or metro.

3. 🇵🇭 Manila/Cebu, Philippines

Manila & Cebu

Why: Flights are cheaper than Thailand if booked right. Islands, diving, beaches, incredible seafood.

Roundtrip flights from LAX: $520–$720

Best time to visit: December–May (dry season)

$15–25/day budget
$40–70/day comfortable

What to do: Boracay (world-class beach), Palawan (El Nido caves), diving in Cebu, Chocolate Hills in Bohol, street food in Manila.

Pro tip: Skip Manila as a base; fly through it to Cebu or Palawan. The real magic is on the islands.

4. 🇻🇳 Hanoi/Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Vietnam (Hanoi & HCMC)

Why: Extremely cheap living. Halong Bay UNESCO site. Street food capital of Southeast Asia.

Roundtrip flights from LAX: $500–$700

Best time to visit: October–March

$12–18/day budget
$35–60/day comfortable

What to do: Halong Bay cruise, Hoi An ancient town, Mekong Delta, water puppets, street food crawls, temples.

Pro tip: Halong Bay day cruises cost $30–50. Hotels $5–15/night. Food $1–3 per meal.

5. 🇨🇴 Bogotá/Medellín, Colombia

Colombia (Bogotá & Medellín)

Why: Underrated. Cheap flights from Miami/Orlando. Coffee, mountains, vibrant cities, friendly people.

Roundtrip flights from MIA: $280–$450

Best time to visit: December–March or July–August (less rain)

$20–30/day budget
$50–80/day comfortable

What to do: Coffee plantations (Eje Cafetero), Comuna 13 street art (Medellín), Salt Cathedral, Monserrate hike (Bogotá), nightlife, trekking.

Pro tip: Bogotá is high altitude (10,000 ft) — allow 2 days to acclimate. Medellín's weather is perfect year-round.

6. 🇵🇹 Lisbon, Portugal

Lisbon

Why: Gateway to Europe. Cheaper than Spain/France. Pastéis de nata, port wine, tiles, hills, nearby beaches.

Roundtrip flights from BOS: $380–$550

Best time to visit: April–May or September–October (warm, fewer crowds)

$25–35/day budget
$60–100/day comfortable

What to do: Pastéis de Nata, Bairro Alto (old neighborhood), São Jorge Castle, Belém Tower, Sintra day trip, Port wine tasting, Cascais beach.

Pro tip: Trams and metro are cheap ($1.50/ride). Restaurants: lunch $5–8, dinner $12–20. Hostels $12–20/night.

7. 🇬🇹 Antigua, Guatemala

Antigua

Why: Colonial charm, Spanish language schools, volcano hikes, Lake Atitlán nearby, ultra-cheap.

Roundtrip flights from ORD: $320–$480

Best time to visit: November–March (dry season)

$18–25/day budget
$40–70/day comfortable

What to do: Spanish classes (immersion), Acatenango volcano hike, Lake Atitlán indigenous markets, coffee plantations, colonial ruins.

Pro tip: Many travelers come for 1-week Spanish courses ($180–250/week). Hotels from $15/night, meals from $2–4.

8. 🇵🇪 Lima/Cusco, Peru

Peru (Lima & Cusco)

Why: Machu Picchu (iconic), Lima has amazing food scene, Sacred Valley, reasonable flights.

Roundtrip flights from LAX: $480–$680

Best time to visit: May–September (dry for hiking)

$20–30/day budget
$50–90/day comfortable

What to do: Machu Picchu trek, Sacred Valley hikes, Cusco colonial architecture, Lima food tours, Amazon jungle, Nazca Lines.

Pro tip: Build in 3+ days for altitude acclimatization in Cusco (11,000 ft). Machu Picchu 3-day treks from $400–600.

9. 🇮🇳 Delhi/Jaipur, India

India (Delhi & Rajasthan)

Why: Legendary cheap travel destination. Taj Mahal, palaces, spirituality, food, chaos (in a good way).

Roundtrip flights from JFK: $600–$900

Best time to visit: October–March (cooler)

$15–20/day budget
$40–70/day comfortable

What to do: Taj Mahal, Jaipur palaces, Delhi street food, Rajasthani forts, yoga retreats, Varanasi temples.

Pro tip: Book trains in advance (more comfortable than it sounds). Hotels from $8–15/night. Food incredibly cheap.

10. 🇪🇨 Quito/Galápagos (Budget Option), Ecuador

Ecuador (Quito & Amazon)

Why: Cheap flights from Miami. Quito is charming and affordable. Amazon and Galápagos nearby (expensive but flights cheaper).

Roundtrip flights from MIA: $300–$500

Best time to visit: October–November (warm, less rain)

$20–30/day budget
$50–80/day comfortable

What to do: Quito colonial center, Otavalo indigenous market, Amazon jungle lodges, cloud forests, volcanoes.

Pro tip: Ecuador uses the US dollar (no currency exchange). Quito hotels $15–30/night. Jungle lodges $40–80/night with meals.
